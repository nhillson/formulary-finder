      How the estimate works

      • It assumes you fill each prescription every month from January through December, in the quantity you enter.
      • Drug prices are the plan's average negotiated price at retail pharmacies in its service area, from Medicare's quarterly pricing files. Dispensing fees aren't included, and your pharmacy's price can differ.
      • Until the deductible is met you pay the full price for drugs on tiers where it applies; after that you pay the plan's copay or coinsurance for the tier. Once your out-of-pocket spending reaches the yearly cap, covered drugs cost $0 for the rest of the year.
      • Covered insulin is capped at $35 a month and isn't subject to the deductible.
      • Drugs a plan doesn't cover are counted at full price and don't count toward the cap. Ask about a formulary exception before ruling a plan out.
      • Medicare Advantage premiums are the plan premium from Medicare's data. Most people also pay the Part B premium, whichever plan they choose.
      • Check your top choices in Medicare's Plan Finder at medicare.gov or by calling 1-800-MEDICARE before you enroll. It uses your exact pharmacy.

      Plan, cost sharing and price data come from Medicare's public Part D files (CMS); drug names from the National Library of Medicine's RxNorm. Updated twice a week. This is an estimate to help you shortlist plans, not a quote.
