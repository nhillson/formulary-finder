Find a cheaper Medicare drug plan

Medicare's website prices plans only for the exact drugs you list. But a small switch, like from rosuvastatin to atorvastatin (two similar cholesterol drugs), or from a brand name to a generic, can make a plan much cheaper. Most drugs have close alternatives, and each plan covers them differently.

This site compares plans with those possible switches included, so you can see where switching might save you money.

Before you act

  • Ask your doctor. Not every suggested switch will be right for you, and any change needs a new prescription.
  • Double-check on Medicare.gov. This site can make mistakes.
  • Your choice, your responsibility. We're not recommending any drug or plan. We're just showing you options.
