      How the estimate works

      • We assume you fill each medicine every month from January through December, at the daily dose you entered.
      • Prices are each plan's average negotiated price at pharmacies in its area, from Medicare's public data. Your pharmacy's price can be different.
      • Until you meet your deductible, you pay the full price for medicines where it applies. After that you pay the plan's copay or percentage. Once you've paid the yearly limit, covered medicines cost $0 for the rest of the year.
      • Covered insulin costs at most $35 a month and isn't subject to the deductible.
      • Medicines a plan doesn't cover are counted at full price. Your doctor can ask the plan for an exception.
      • Switching to a different strength or a similar medicine needs a new prescription. Always talk with your doctor or pharmacist first.
      • Before you enroll, check your top choices on Medicare's Plan Finder at medicare.gov or call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227). They use your exact pharmacy.

      Plan, cost and price data come from Medicare's public Part D files (CMS), and medicine names from the National Library of Medicine's RxNorm. Updated twice a week. This is an estimate to help you choose, not a price quote.
